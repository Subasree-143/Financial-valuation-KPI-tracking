# Intel Financial Valuation & KPI Tracking

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Intel Corporation's financial performance and key business metrics using publicly available SEC 10-K filings from 2020 through 2024.

The analysis was developed as a financial and strategic decision-making exercise, using historical financial statements to evaluate revenue trends, profitability, operating performance, financial position, and key performance indicators.

The project combines SEC filings with Excel-based financial analysis and valuation models to translate historical financial information into actionable business insights.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze Intel's financial performance across multiple years.
* Track changes in revenue, profitability, expenses, assets, liabilities, and other financial indicators.
* Calculate and interpret key financial and operating performance metrics.
* Evaluate year-over-year changes and identify significant trends.
* Apply financial valuation and forecasting concepts.
* Use historical financial information to support business and investment-oriented decision-making.
* Present financial analysis in a structured and decision-oriented format.

---

## Methodology

The project follows a structured financial analysis workflow:

### 1. Data Collection

Historical financial information was collected from Intel's annual Form 10-K filings for fiscal years 2020–2024.

The filings provide information related to:

* Consolidated statements of operations
* Balance sheet information
* Cash flows
* Revenue
* Operating expenses
* Assets and liabilities
* Shareholders' equity
* Segment and business performance
* Other financial and operational disclosures

### 2. Financial Statement Analysis

Historical financial statement information was organized and analyzed to understand changes in Intel's financial position and operating performance over time.

The analysis focuses on identifying:

* Revenue growth or decline
* Changes in operating costs
* Profitability trends
* Changes in asset and liability balances
* Cash flow movements
* Changes in financial efficiency

### 3. KPI Analysis

Key performance indicators were calculated and evaluated to assess Intel's business performance.

Examples of metrics analyzed include:

* Revenue growth
* Gross margin
* Operating margin
* Net margin
* Earnings-related metrics
* Expense ratios
* Return-based measures
* Liquidity indicators
* Leverage-related measures
* Cash flow indicators

### 4. Trend Analysis

Financial metrics were compared across multiple fiscal years to identify:

* Positive and negative performance trends
* Significant changes in financial results
* Periods of improving or declining performance
* Potential operational and strategic factors affecting results

### 5. Financial Valuation

Financial valuation concepts were applied using historical company performance and financial assumptions.

The valuation work evaluates financial performance and uses structured assumptions to support an assessment of the company's financial outlook.

### 6. Excel-Based Modeling

Excel was used as the primary analytical environment.

The workbooks contain financial calculations, supporting analysis, formulas, and structured financial models used throughout the project.

* The analysis is based primarily on historical financial information.
* Financial performance does not guarantee future results.
* Valuation outputs are dependent on the assumptions used in the underlying models.
* SEC filings may contain changes in accounting classifications or reporting structures across years, which can affect direct comparisons.
* The project should be viewed as an analytical and educational exercise rather than investment advice.
